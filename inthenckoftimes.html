2L NSCLC, the cancer type we're targeting first.

April 2027

Target: First patient dosed

Q2 2027 is our milestone for first-in-human dosing of a novel combination. Getting there requires regulatory engagement, study design, PK/tox data, and defensible IP, but above all, conviction that our combo delivers meaningful efficacy at a tolerable dose. Every experiment we run is in service of that moment.

Rapid evolutionary experimental cycles, Oncko's 2026 engine.

January 2026

2026: Evolving the platform

We're sharpening our focus on 2L NSCLC — patients who've already exhausted frontline therapy. These tumors are resistant, heterogeneous, and badly served by standard preclinical models. Oncko is built to meet them where they are.

Year 2 targets:

  • Nomination strategy: 1 → 10 orthogonal approaches evaluated in parallel
  • Evidence base: in vitro only → layered clinical and mechanistic data
  • Xenograft coverage: 10 combos → 100
  • Program maturity: 1 early screen → 2 pharma/investor-grade packages with defensible IP

An Oncko combos outperforming SoC across 6 independent xenograft models.

November 2025

Milestones hit. More funding closed.

We hit our key 2025 targets: engineered 2 drug combos beating standard-of-care TGI by >70% across multiple xenograft models, demonstrated that in vitro screens predict clinical combo outcomes, and closed additional funding. Great advisory board is in place.

AI × biology event we hosted bringing together 100 investors, operators, and scientists.

February 2025

2025 Milestones: and JPM event

We published our 2025 targets: prove in vitro combo screens are reproducible and predict PDX and clinical outcomes; achieve >50% improvement over standard of care in vivo. Alongside that, our AI x bio side event at JPM drew 100 attendees: pharma, investors, founders, and AI researchers in one room.

The napkin sketch that started it all, SoMa SF, summer 2024.

November 2024

Napkins to SAFEs

A few months of strategy sessions and early fundraising led to a signed SAFE in November, the starting gun for Oncko. The thesis that started on a cocktail napkin in San Francisco now had capital behind it.
