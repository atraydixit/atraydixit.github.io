2L NSCLC, the cancer type we're targeting first.

April 2027

Target: First patient dosed

Q2 2027 is our milestone for first-in-human dosing of a novel combination. Getting there requires regulatory engagement, study design, PK/tox data, and defensible IP, but above all, conviction that our combo delivers meaningful efficacy at a tolerable dose. Every experiment we run is in service of that moment.

Rapid evolutionary experimental cycles, Oncko's 2026 engine.

January 2026

2026: Scale, Evolution, Translation

We're sharpening our focus on 2L NSCLC, patients who've already exhausted frontline therapy. These tumors are resistant, heterogeneous, and badly served by standard preclinical models. Oncko is built to meet them where they are.

Year 1 → Year 2 targets:

Evidence base: in vitro only → layered clinical and mechanistic data

Scale of testing: 10 → 100 combos tested in vivo

Program maturity: 1 early screen → 2 pharma/investor-grade packages with defensible IP

Our combo outperforming standard of care across 6 xenograft models.

November 2025

Milestones hit. More funding closed.

We hit our key 2025 targets: engineered 2 drug combos beating drugs currently given to 2L lung cancer patients by >70% across multiple mouse model of human cancer, demonstrated that in vitro screens predict clinical combo outcomes, and closed additional funding.

AI × biology event we hosted bringing together 100 investors, operators, and scientists.

February 2025

2025 Milestones and JPM event

We set our 2025 targets: prove in vitro combo screens are reproducible and predict clinical outcomes; achieve >50% improvement over the drugs currently given to cancer patients (standard of care) in mouse models of cancer (xenografts). Alongside that, our AI x bio side event at JPM drew 100 attendees: pharma, investors, founders, and AI researchers in one room.

Napkin sketch (June 2024) and our 1st signed investment (November 2024)

November 2024

Napkins to SAFEs

A few months of strategy sessions and early fundraising led to closing a lead investor in November, the starting gun for Oncko. The thesis that started on a cocktail napkin in San Francisco now had capital behind it.
