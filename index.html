Combination intelligence

Engineering cancer drug combinations.

Cancer evolves resistance quickly. Drug combinations are among the most effective treatments. But finding the right drugs, doses, and timing is a massive search problem. Oncko is building the platform to solve it.

Problem

The search space is the bottleneck.

Drugs x Dose x Schedule x Cancer Biology

Even with a limited starting set of oncology agents, the number of possible combinations can be in the millions to billions. Traditional workflows explore only a tiny fraction of this space, making it hard to identify regimens that maximize efficacy while controlling toxicity. In contrast, some of the most successful cancer regimens are multi-drug combos.

How we solve it

A platform for combination discovery and optimization.

We generate large-scale experimental data on drug combinations, evaluate efficacy and toxicity, and use that signal to guide optimization toward better regimens.

01

Screen against SuperTumor™

Combos are tested in SuperTumor™, a preclinical model that represents a large diversity of post-treatment cancer biology.

02

Measure efficacy and toxicity together

Capture the tradeoff that determines whether a candidate is worth advancing.

03

Optimize toward stronger regimens

Search toward combinations with improved activity and lower expected toxicity.
