Scaling combination testing

A new frontier in combinations.

Cancer and infectious diseases evolve resistance to single drugs. Drug combinations have made many of these conditions survivable. Breakthroughs in biology and AI keep producing new therapies, but each one expands the combination space exponentially.

Informed by large human and preclinical datasets, Oncko is building ComboTrials to search that space and find the combinations that matter.

Learn about ComboTrials

The space

Historically intractable.

In 1980 there were 13 approved classes of cancer drug. Today there are 82, making over 90,000 possible pairs and triples — and that space doubles about every six years. Most trials still test them one at a time.

Registered trial arms (ClinicalTrials.gov) on an 82-class solid-tumour grid; the 1980 panel is literature-sourced on 8 chemotherapy classes. Oncko layer illustrative.

Trials & Partnering

Our clinical trials.

One protocol, one shared randomized control, one operational team, and a set of preclinically validated backbones to combine with. A partner arm joins the platform instead of standing up a new trial, so a combination reaches human proof faster and at a fraction of what a standalone trial would cost.
