Scaling trials for drug combinations.

Many diseases, including cancer, are too complex for or evolve resistance to single drugs and benefit from combination therapy. Breakthroughs in biology and AI keep producing new therapies. Each one adds to an exponentially growing number of combinations worth testing.

Informed by large human and preclinical datasets, Oncko is building ComboTrials to search that space and find the combinations that matter.

Learn about ComboTrials

The space

Historically intractable.

In 1980 there were 8 drug classes for cancer. Today there are 82, making over 90,000 possible pairs and triples — and that space doubles about every five years.

Trials & Partnering

ComboTrials programs.

Platform clinical trials designed to scale combination testing. Built for teams exploring new combinations in lung, colorectal or pancreatic cancer.

One protocol, one shared randomized control, one operational team, and a set of prioritized backbones to combine with. A partner arm joins the platform instead of standing up a new trial, which is what shortens the path to human proof.
